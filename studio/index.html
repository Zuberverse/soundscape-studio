A small studio for sound and scenes

  1. Create, upload, or select a reusable frame.
  2. Add sound and choose movement. A scene combines a frame, sound, and direction.
  3. Connect your Comfy account, review the run, then create and watch your scene here.

Comfy supplies the generation. Your connected workspace needs execution access and credits. Soundscape Studio has no shared paid key and does not buy plans.

Want to use an agent instead? “Use an agent” copies a brief for Codex or Claude. Native Comfy workflows are also available under “Other ways to create.”
